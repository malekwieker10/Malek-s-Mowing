Questions

Good to know

What areas do you serve?

Aberdeen and the surrounding areas. If you're a little way out of town, ask anyway — send your address and we'll tell you straight.

Do I need to be home?

No. As long as gates are unlocked and pets are inside, we'll take care of it while you're out.

What happens if it rains?

We shift you to the next dry day and text you the new time. You're never charged for a visit that didn't happen.

How do I pay?

Cash, check, or Venmo. Regular customers can settle up monthly if that's easier — just say the word.

Do you haul away the clippings?

Standard cuts are mulched back into the lawn, which feeds the turf. If the grass is tall or you'd rather have it bagged and hauled, we can do that for a small add-on.

Am I locked into a contract?

Never. Weekly and seasonal customers can pause or cancel with a week's notice.
